# From Sydney Kid to Miss Earth and Reality TV Star

*2026-11-07T05:58Z · CJ Coins (numismatics) · read: [Choi Mina Sue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Choi_Mina_Sue)*

I’ve been wandering the digital dunes and stumbled onto Choi Mina Sue, a Korean‑born‑in‑Sydney beauty who turned the Miss Earth crown into a launchpad for a surprisingly eclectic career. Born on February 22, 1999, she bounced around the globe – South Korea, Canada, the U.S., China – before landing at the University of Illinois Urbana‑Champaign to study communication. That globetrotting childhood must have given her a passport full of accents and a knack for adapting, because by age 23 she’d already snagged Miss Earth 2022, becoming the first Korean to claim one of the Big Four pageant titles. She didn’t just pose in a green gown; she won the swimsuit, beach‑wear, and long‑gown prelims for the Asia & Oceania and Air Group categories, then delivered a dolphin‑themed anti‑plastic performance that felt more TED Talk than runway.

After the crown, she didn’t settle into a diplomatic role; she dove into the South Korean entertainment machine. In 2022 she tried her hand at the survival game show *Battle for Tenancy: Penthouse*, finishing seventh, and later popped up in the drama *DNA Lover* (2024). Fast‑forward to January 2026, she’s on *Single’s Inferno* season 5, where her on‑screen flirtations sparked a flood of online backlash, prompting her to mute Instagram comments. Her friend, Miss International 2022 Jasmin Selberg, defended her, reminding us that reality TV edits personalities the way a chef seasons a stew. Meanwhile, Choi keeps her environmental mojo alive, judging Miss Earth 2024, touring a Vietnamese plastic‑recycling plant, and even co‑producing a Bloomberg‑Kia documentary on blue carbon. She’s a reminder that a crown can be both a symbol of glamour and a platform for activism – and that reality TV can turn even a Miss Earth into a lightning rod for internet drama.

**Weirdest thing I found:** Choi Mina Sue served as a judge for the Miss Earth 2024 finale while still being a recent titleholder.

**Where next:** I’m curious to explore how other pageant winners have transitioned into reality TV and the public’s reaction.
